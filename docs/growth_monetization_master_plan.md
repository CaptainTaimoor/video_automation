# Growth + Monetization Master Plan
**Channels:** Secrets of Time (`ancient_history`) · Brain Lens (`brain_lens`)  
**Created:** 2026-09-06  
**Goal:** Grow audience fast, hit YouTube monetization, then stack real income.

---

## 0) Direct answer on “post every hour”

**Your idea is half-right.**

- **More posts can grow audience** — Shorts discovery likes consistent volume.
- **But “every hour Short + every 2h Long” forever is usually wrong.**

### Why not full blast forever
1. **YouTube cares about watch quality**, not upload count. Bad Shorts train the algorithm to stop pushing you.
2. **Brain Lens retention ~32%** — flooding hurts more than it helps until hooks/captions improve.
3. **Secrets of Time retention ~77%** — can handle higher volume *if quality stays high*.
4. **PC + API limits** — every-hour Shorts *and* every-2h longs on **two** channels ≈ 48 Shorts + 24 longs/day. Builds will queue, fail, or ship weak videos.
5. **Best posting times** are real — but you learn them from **winning videos**, not from dumping weak ones at every hour.

### What we should do instead (agreed middle path)
| Phase | Secrets of Time | Brain Lens |
|---|---|---|
| **A – Quality lock (7 days)** | Keep strong quality; raise Shorts volume moderately | **Fewer** Shorts; fix first 2 seconds hard |
| **B – Volume surge (14 days)** | High Shorts volume (near hourly in peak windows) | Raise volume **only if** retention ≥ ~50% |
| **C – Time optimize** | Keep winners’ hours; cut losers | Same |
| **D – Money stack** | Longs + cross-post + affiliates | Same |

**Rule:** Volume is a **lever after quality floors**. Never trade retention for “more posts.”

## 3) Channel playbooks

### 3.1 Secrets of Time (strong retention → scale)
**Win pattern already:** evidence / artifact / Why-How-Could titles; ~77% retention.

**Do more of**
- Map / artifact / battle hooks in first **2 seconds** (on-screen place + year)
- Titles: How / Why / Could / “pieces of evidence…”
- Shorts **50–59s**
- Visual clarity: one claim, labeled visuals, motion every 3–5s

**Do less of**
- Soft intros, vague mysteries, static collage loops
- Posting weak builds just to hit a slot

**Volume plan**
- Phase A: **8–10 Shorts/day** + **2–3 longs/day** (close to current cap)
- Phase B (if retention stays ≥65%): surge to **12–16 Shorts/day** in **06:00–23:00** (≈ every 60–75 min in waking hours — *not* dead night spam)
- Longs: **every 3–4 hours** in daytime (not every 2h overnight) so encode queue stays healthy
- Phase C: keep top 8–10 hours only

### 3.2 Brain Lens (weak retention → fix first)
**Problem:** people leave early → algorithm stops testing you.

**Fix order (non-negotiable)**
1. On-screen claim in **second 0–1**
2. Spoken hook by **second 2**
3. Huge mid-frame captions
4. One idea only (How/Why + one tip)
5. Face-first or clean graphic panel (not busy stock soup)

**Volume plan**
- Phase A: **4–6 Shorts/day** + **1–2 longs/day** (quality > quantity)
- Gate to raise volume: rolling 7-day retention **≥ 50%** and engaged-view **≥ 35%**
- Phase B: then **8–12 Shorts/day**
- If retention drops below 45% after surge → **cut volume immediately**

## 4) Posting / scheduling plan (phased)

### Phase A — Days 1–7: “Quality lock”
**Goal:** stop teaching YouTube bad habits.

| Channel | Shorts/day | Longs/day | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Secrets of Time | 8–10 | 2–3 | Keep competitor-optimized slots; fill gaps only with strong backlog |
| Brain Lens | 4–6 | 1–2 | Peak hours only (09, 10, 14, 17, 22-ish) |

**Bot actions**
- Raise quality gates for Brain (reject weak hooks / low caption CPS / dark frames)
- Force first-second title card for Brain
- Restart scheduler once after config change (graceful)

**Daily review (5–10 min)**
- Dashboard: retention, upload gaps, failures
- Kill repeating weak title patterns

### Phase B — Days 8–21: “Volume surge” (your idea, controlled)
**Goal:** maximize discovery **without** trash.

**Secrets of Time surge schedule (example target)**
- Shorts: roughly **hourly from 07:00–23:00** (≈ 16–17 slots) — skip 01:00–06:00 unless data proves those hours win
- Longs: **09:00, 12:00, 15:00, 18:00, 21:00** (5/day max) — *not* 12 longs/day

**Brain Lens surge** — only if Phase A gates pass:
- Shorts every **90–120 min** in peak windows (≈ 8–12/day)
- Longs 2–3/day

**Why not true 24× Shorts + 12× Longs**
- Encode time + API + topic uniqueness will break
- Night posts with low retention poison the channel
- Better: **dense daytime volume** + measure

### Phase C — Days 22–35: “Best-time trim”
**Goal:** keep winners, cut losers.

Method:
1. Pull performance report (views, retention, publish hour)
2. Rank hours
3. Keep top **60–70%** of slots
4. Permanently drop bottom hours
5. Optionally keep 1–2 “probe” slots/week to re-test

### Phase D — Day 36+: “Money mode”
- Shorts stay as growth engine at optimized cadence
- Longs prioritized for watch hours + ad RPM
- Every Short description CTA → best related long
- Cross-post pipeline on
- Affiliate / brand experiments start

## 8) Technical / ops plan (so volume doesn’t break the bot)

### Hard constraints on this PC
- Prefer QSV encode; avoid overload
- Cap concurrent builds (1 active render recommended)
- Pre-build backlog overnight; upload on schedule by day
- Daily caps must match **successful quality uploads**, not attempted slots

### Recommended bot upgrades (implementation backlog)
1. **Prebuild queue:** build N Shorts ahead; scheduler only uploads
2. **Dynamic volume controller:** auto raise/cut posts from retention KPI
3. **Hourly daytime mode** for Ancient only (flag in `controls/`)
4. **Best-hour learner:** weekly rewrite `schedule_times` from analytics
5. **Cross-post module:** YT → FB (then TikTok)
6. **Short→Long linker:** auto description + pinned comment
7. **Failure budget:** if >X% builds fail/day, pause surge
8. **Topic uniqueness:** prevent near-duplicate spam during surge

### Safety
- Never hard-kill mid-render
- Graceful scheduler restart after schedule changes
- Keep quality gates on during surge (no “upload anything”)
